KEY FINDINGS (from conversation)
================================================================================

1. PURCHASING totals: App output (8,124) vs Odoo actual (8,203) = ~1% gap. Acceptable.

2. SALES totals: App output is ~30% off from actual. Root cause under investigation.

3. FIELD MAPPING ISSUES IDENTIFIED:
   - App uses "effective date" — should possibly use "ordered date"
   - App uses "delivered quantity" — should possibly use "ordered quantity"
   - App uses "price subtotal" — should possibly use "delivering subtotal"

4. FILTER ISSUES IDENTIFIED:
   - App may not be excluding invoices in "draft" or "cancelled" status
   - App may not be filtering by income account type (cuenta de ingresos)
   - App may be including archived records inconsistently
   - App confused Product 1045 (cuchara) with 1046 (vaso) — merged as same product

5. PROPOSED FIX (from David):
   - Filter by income account type (cuenta de ingresos/gastos)
   - Filter where type = "ingreso"
   - Exclude invoices where status = "borrador" OR "cancelado"
   - Ensure archived products are handled consistently
   - Ensure product IDs 1045 and 1046 are treated as distinct products
