Correct purchases_ordered SSOT: exclude unconfirmed and cancelled POs

Establishes the DEMO-scope definition of a Purchase Order (confirmed
2026-04-28): state IN ('purchase', 'locked', 'done') only.

Excluded states and reasons:
  - draft: not yet sent to supplier
  - solicitud de cotización / sent: RFQ submitted to supplier but not
    yet confirmed as a purchase order
  - cancel: not a purchase order; no goods ever committed or received
  - to approve: never used by the company in DEMO or PROD; no plans to
    use it in the future; absent from the production purchase_orders table

Root cause this fixes: the original SSOT label pol_all_states_* included
cancelled POs (319 records, 300,823 units, 0 received) and unconfirmed
RFQs (29 records, 7,987 units, 0 received) in the purchases_ordered
training data. The ML model learned purchase patterns from volume that
was never committed and never arrived, directly inflating yhat_sum
predictions. This is a confirmed root cause of the impossibly large
Compras Ordenadas values reported by the insider on 2026-04-28.
