WHY THE RE-TRAINING
revenue_daily_for_ml was rebuilt 2026-04-29 (computed_at range
04:18–13:18 UTC), five days after the sales Prophet forecasts were
written on 2026-04-24T17:29 UTC. The training data had changed, so
all prior forecast cells were stale. Re-ran the full two-pass pipeline
against the current data:
  Pass 1 — Prophet (sales): all 23 demo SKUs, training 2024-10-01 to
            2026-01-31, prediction Feb + Mar 2026.
  Pass 2 — Derived ratio (purchases_ordered + purchases_received):
            sales_forecast × median(PO/Sales ratio, Tukey IQR×1.5
            outlier exclusion), per SKU.
Result: 138 forecast cells confirmed (23 SKUs × 3 metrics × 2 months),
all model_status = ok / ok_derived.

PO HISTORY COMPLETENESS COLUMN
14 of 23 demo SKUs have fewer than 3 months of confirmed purchase order
history in revenue_daily_for_ml (SSOT pol_confirmed_date_planned_
product_qty_c40, states purchase/locked/done). The derived purchase
forecast for these SKUs is based on 2 onboarding months only (Oct–Nov
2024) and should be read with caution. Root cause: the company procures
several CARVAJAL imported products through a mechanism not captured in
standard Odoo PO tables for most of the training window.

Migration adds po_history_real_months SMALLINT (0–16) to
products_acid_test_active, recording the count of calendar months in
the 16-month training window that have at least one confirmed PO.
Synthetic fallback data (find_16) does not count. Backfill values
verified by direct query of revenue_daily_for_ml 2026-05-05:
  GREEN  (16/16): 13 SKUs — pids 5,20,29,33,36,145,469,539,
                            1113,1127,1366,1562,1606
  AMBER  ( 5/16):  2 SKUs — pids 37, 1035
  RED    ( 2/16):  8 SKUs — pids 2,3,34,1069,1096,1587,1590,1600
Amber/red boundary (≥3 months) is a provisional proxy pending Acid
Test 2 scoring against Luis's actual Feb/Mar 2026 values.
